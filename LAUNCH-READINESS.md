## Launch gates

The platform should not be described as fully operational until the unchecked production dependencies have been implemented, tested and verified.

## Legal/organizational

Before launch, each participating entity should confirm its own:
- registration and governance
- fundraising permissions
- tax treatment
- data protection obligations
- safeguarding obligations
- grant restrictions
- cross-border transfer requirements

The platform does not replace those obligations.
