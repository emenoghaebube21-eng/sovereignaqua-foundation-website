# SovereignAqua Imperium-Habour — Global Institute Architecture

## Global positioning

SovereignAqua Imperium-Habour is being structured as a worldwide institutional platform for collaboration among nonprofit entities, NGOs, humanitarian organizations, foundations, community organizations, research institutions, social-impact enterprises, donors, investors, volunteers, and affiliated organizations.

The platform provides digital coordination, institutional profiles, project collaboration, documentation, research, resource sharing, and partnership workflows.

It does not by itself confer charitable, nonprofit, tax-exempt, NGO, diplomatic, governmental, or international legal status. Each participating entity retains its own legal identity and remains subject to the law and regulatory requirements applicable to it.

## Verification

Organization profiles should distinguish:
- Self-declared information
- Platform-reviewed information
- Third-party verification
- Official registration evidence
- Partnership status

Verification badges must describe the actual verification performed and must never imply government accreditation or universal legal recognition without evidence.

## Global governance

The platform should support regional and country-level structures while maintaining consistent technical standards:
- Global administration
- Regional coordination
- Country/community coordination
- Program leadership
- Project teams

Regional structures must remain subject to applicable local law and the governing instruments of the participating organizations.

## Humanitarian principles

Humanitarian workflows should be designed around:
- Humanity
- Impartiality
- Neutrality
- Independence
- Do-no-harm principles
- Safeguarding
- Data minimization
- Protection of vulnerable people

Sensitive beneficiary information should not be placed in public profiles.
