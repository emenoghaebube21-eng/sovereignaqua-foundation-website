# Donor & Grantor Guide

## Support options

Support may be structured as:
- voluntary donation
- restricted project contribution
- voluntary grant
- institutional grant
- in-kind contribution
- humanitarian emergency support

## Before funding

The platform should show:
- receiving entity
- project/program
- funding purpose
- restrictions
- agreement requirements
- reporting expectations
- payment processor

## After funding

Authorized administrators can record:
- receipt
- allocation
- disbursement
- milestones
- project reports
- closeout

Donor privacy must be respected. Public reporting should not expose private financial information without appropriate authorization.

## Foreign partner

Where SovereignAqua Living Trust Foundation participates, the parties should document:
- roles
- receiving entity
- fund custody
- permitted uses
- reporting
- audit rights
- currency and transfer arrangements
- termination/closeout
- applicable law
