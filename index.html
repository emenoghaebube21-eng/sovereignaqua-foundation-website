THE MANDATE

Private structure. Public-purpose impact.

Imperium-Habour PMA is designed as an organized private community with defined membership, governance, programs, records, and service pathways. Its operating instruments and applicable law control the rights and obligations of participants.

01 / CHARTER

Foundational architecture

01

Purpose

Coordinate members and resources around water, environmental, infrastructure, research, and sustainable-development initiatives.

02

Principles

Integrity, stewardship, responsible administration, documented authority, transparency of process, and disciplined recordkeeping.

03

Instruments

Membership terms, governance documents, project agreements, policies, records, and other controlling instruments adopted by the PMA.

02 / MEMBERSHIP

A defined member experience.

Membership can be organized around eligibility, application, verification, orientation, participation, records, and renewal procedures established by the PMA.
