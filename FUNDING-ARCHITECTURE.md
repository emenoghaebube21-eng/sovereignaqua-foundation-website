# Global Donations & Voluntary Grant Architecture

## Purpose

SovereignAqua Imperium-Habour may operate a platform for receiving and administering voluntary donations, grants, sponsorships, and other lawful philanthropic support for approved humanitarian, research, environmental, water, infrastructure, community-development, and related projects.

Acceptance and use of funds must follow the entity's governing instruments, applicable law, donor restrictions, banking/payment-provider requirements, sanctions and AML obligations where applicable, and any agreements governing a particular project.

## Funding channels

- Voluntary donations
- Restricted project donations
- Unrestricted philanthropic support
- Voluntary grants
- Institutional grants
- Corporate/social-impact sponsorships
- In-kind contributions
- Program-specific funding
- Emergency humanitarian support

## Donor workflow

Donor → Funding intent → Eligibility/compliance checks where required → Donation/grant record → Allocation → Project implementation → Reporting → Closeout

## Grant workflow

Grant proposal → Review → Approval → Grant agreement → Disbursement → Milestones → Reporting → Reconciliation → Closeout

## Foreign partner relationship

The platform may record a formal relationship with **SovereignAqua Living Trust Foundation** as a foreign partner where an appropriate written agreement establishes the relationship.

The platform should distinguish:
- partner
- fiscal/grant administrator
- implementing organization
- project sponsor
- donor
- recipient
- beneficiary

The website must not imply that funds received by one entity automatically belong to, are controlled by, or are tax-deductible through another entity.

## Transparency

For each funding record, where appropriate:
- funding source/category
- amount and currency
- restriction status
- designated project
- agreement/reference
- receipt date
- disbursement status
- reporting status

Public reporting should use aggregated information where donor or beneficiary privacy requires it.

## Important legal and financial boundary

Whether a donation or grant is tax-deductible, charitable, exempt, restricted, reportable, or otherwise regulated depends on the receiving entity, jurisdiction, donor, funding instrument, and applicable law. The platform should not label a payment "tax deductible" or "charitable" without appropriate basis.

Payment processing should be performed through compliant payment providers. Never store card numbers, banking passwords, private keys, or payment-provider secrets in the website repository.
