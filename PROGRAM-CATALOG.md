# Global Program Catalog

## Water & sanitation
Access, infrastructure, monitoring, resilience and community water initiatives.

## Environmental stewardship
Conservation, restoration, land/resource research and environmental education.

## Climate resilience
Preparedness, adaptation, resilience and community response.

## Food security
Agriculture, fisheries, nutrition, livelihoods and food-system resilience.

## Health & wellbeing
Community health, health education, wellbeing and humanitarian health support.

## Education & skills
Education access, vocational development, digital skills and professional training.

## Infrastructure
Community infrastructure, housing support, utilities and resilient systems.

## Humanitarian response
Emergency response, relief logistics, recovery and preparedness.

## Research & innovation
Applied research, technology, data, engineering and knowledge exchange.

## Youth & community development
Youth participation, community leadership and local capacity building.

Every program should have defined objectives, responsible entities, governance, safeguarding, funding rules and impact indicators before public launch.
